Health Knowledge

Common Myths vs. Medical Facts

M Myth: Donating blood makes you physically weak or sick.

Medical Fact: Your body naturally replenishes the lost fluid volume within 24–48 hours, and new red blood cells are generated within a few weeks. Regular blood donation actually stimulates new cell growth and reduces harmful excess iron!

M Myth: You can catch infections like HIV or Hepatitis by donating blood.

Medical Fact: Completely impossible. A brand-new, sterile, disposable needle and bag set is used for every single donor and incinerated immediately afterward.

M Myth: Women cannot donate blood.

Medical Fact: Any healthy female aged 18–60 with a weight of 48kg+ and normal hemoglobin (>=12.0 g/dL) can safely donate blood every 4 months.
